How I built it

Guelph Solar AI Sales Assistant

Built in a 2-person team during my internship. A call comes in, Gemini analyses it, a sales rep reviews it, and Make.com keeps the rest of the sales tools in sync.

1

Call comes in

Phone recording or Google Meet transcript, linked to a customer record.

2

Express API

Node.js and Express endpoints save customers, conversations, and transcripts in PostgreSQL.

3

Gemini 2.5 Flash

Summarises the call and marks the transcript lines it used as evidence.

4

Rep reviews

Next.js screens show the analysis beside the transcript; the rep edits and approves it.
